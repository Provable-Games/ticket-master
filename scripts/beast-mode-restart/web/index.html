Migration sequence

PLAN · NO MAINNET CALLDATA

Deployment and cutover require separate reviewed transactions. These steps describe the intended sequence.

  1. Reconcile the LORDS setup

    If the original sale has been restarted, stop it and recover remaining tickets and liquidity. Reconcile old LORDS proceeds and settle outstanding buyback obligations before moving their NFT.

  2. Finalize the new controller

    Keep administration with the founding multisig. Decide how issuance automation and emergency recovery should work, then review the production implementation.

  3. Create the SURVIVOR pool and order

    Use the same D-ticket token, a separate SURVIVOR trading pair, new position NFTs, fee 0, the existing full range and TWAMM extension. Preserve the intended active issuance rate and original end date.

  4. Refresh price and funding

    Convert the intended LORDS ticket price into SURVIVOR using a fresh quote. Confirm the seed budget and recalculate the tick, ticket inventory and minimum amounts before preparing calls.

  5. Fund and hand over the positions

    Deploy the finalized controller and place the new order and LP NFTs in its custody. The founding multisig remains owner of the D-ticket contract and controller.

  6. Verify payouts and resume operation

    Send 80% of newly claimed SURVIVOR directly to DAO; convert 20% through a LORDS buy order for veLords. Verify purchases, conversion claims, purchase routing and the chosen keeper behavior.
